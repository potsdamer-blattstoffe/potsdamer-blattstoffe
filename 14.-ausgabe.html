On Blue and Writing Poetry
    
From the shrine

The icon of Mary in blue was brought down
And on the grass, she was laid to rest.

I don’t believe this myself nor will I soon 
Know whether these gestures of grace

Have started to take hold in the lines
Exhausted and as content as they are

On her face.

And turning on colors are starched leaves
And how irrevocably starched they are

And they go on turning with nothing to say.
The hues of dawn are finally washed

And each midway, each valley is hell
But emptied of beasts and as repentant as

We could be.

The sinful and infirm are who should be,
In their own way, some old scarecrow

From whom the images have been revoked,
To whom images were everything

And are icons now, sweet delicacies,
And not one of them can remember you.
