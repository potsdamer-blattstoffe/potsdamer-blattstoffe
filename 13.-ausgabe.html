Simon Chenard in the Costume of a Sans-Culotte:
“Well, where am I? What do I wear?
Nothing but sans-culotte.
Is that a hill to die upon?
Or not?”
    
Louis-Léopold Boilly, Portrait du chanteur Simon Chenard en costume de sans-culotte
    c. 1792. Oil on panel, Musée Carnavalet, Paris. Image: Wikimedia Commons
