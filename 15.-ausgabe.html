15. Ausgabe - Oktober 2026 / 15th Issue - October 2026

stones mate under the whistling skies
a bubbling conceit of dawn
no apparition or remembrance
porcelain scorn. labial luxuries
forlorn the prophet and detestable
his sores
hands of a clay we taste and
smack our lips and mourn and hate
a handbook full of undulating porn
some fatty solids break the sky
descend and kiss me on the nose
they’re just a bit of nothing more
than my forgotten violence
washed ashore
    
    
***

    
the little jerks and jolts, the rubble on the floor announce
today the grand french gruesome characters arrive,
arrange my objects in the dawn,
mixing light stone cucked fruit
in slime around our crackle house.
she pops her finger in my mouth.
snap on the wrist. boom-bust.
a cute gelatinous cocoon
accepts my penant puddle in the dusk.
miserable miracle achieved honeyweek night
i fall asleep soaked in my clear pale desperate delight.
